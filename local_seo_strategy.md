## 4. Map Ranking Strategy (30-60 Days)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-15)

1. **Claim & Verify:** Ensure GBP is verified.
2. **Complete Profile:** Add all services (Airport Shuttle, Limo Service, Tour Agency).
3. **Photos:** Upload 20+ high-quality photos of your *actual* vehicles with license plates visible (adds trust) and team members.
4. **Citations:** Register on top 10 Moroccan directories (NAP consistency: Name, Address, Phone must be identical).

### Phase 2: Activity (Days 16-30)

1. **Reviews:** actively use the WhatsApp templates to get your first 10-20 reviews. **Reply to every review** within 24 hours including keywords (e.g., "Thanks for booking your *Marrakech private driver* with us").
2. **Posts:** Start posting twice a week (Offer + Team photo).
3. **Q&A:** Populate your GBP Q&A section with common questions (e.g., "Do you offer child seats?" "Yes, our *private transport service* includes free child seats").

### Phase 3: Authority (Days 31-60)

1. **Backlinks:** secure 3-5 backlinks from local Riads or travel blogs using the template above.
2. **Embed Map:** Ensure the Google Map is embedded on your `contact.html` and `index.html` (Footer).
3. **Velocity:** Aim for a steady stream of 2-3 reviews per week rather than a sudden spike.

**Goal:** reaching the "Local Pack" (Top 3) for "Private Driver Marrakech" and "Airport Transfer Casablanca".
