Discover the Magic of the Blue City

MouTaouakil Transfers offers premium private transport to Chefchaouen, the world-famous "Blue Pearl" nestled in the Rif Mountains. Whether you are traveling from Fes, Tangier, or Casablanca, our luxury vehicles ensure a smooth and scenic journey through the mountains.

Chefchaouen is a photographer's paradise, known for its blue-washed walls, narrow alleys, and relaxed atmosphere. With a private driver, you can enjoy the stunning drive, stop for panoramic photos, and arrive refreshed and ready to explore.

Popular Transfer Routes

  • Fes to Chefchaouen: A beautiful 3.5 to 4-hour drive through the Rif Mountains. We can stop at the Volubilis Roman ruins on the way.
  • Tangier to Chefchaouen: A shorter 2-hour drive, perfect for a day trip or transfer upon arrival at Tangier Port.
  • Casablanca to Chefchaouen: A longer journey (approx. 5 hours) made comfortable by our luxury fleet and professional drivers.

Why Book a Private Driver?

  • Safety & Comfort: Experienced drivers who know the mountain roads well.
  • Flexibility: Stop for photos, coffee, or rest whenever you wish.
  • Door-to-Door Service: We drop you as close as possible to your accommodation.
