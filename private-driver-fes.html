Private Chauffeur for City Tours

Uncover the secrets of the Medina and beyond with our private chauffeur for city tours. Fes poses logistical challenges for visitors, but a private car allows you to move between the sprawling historic sites with ease and comfort.

Your dedicated driver can take you to the magnificent Royal Palace gates, the panoramic Merinid Tombs for a sunset view over the city, and the famous Chouara Tannery. Visit the ancient Al Quaraouiyine University and explore the Jewish Quarter (Mellah) without the fatigue of walking long distances between sites.

Our private service offers the flexibility to linger at sites that interest you most, from ceramic workshops to intricate madrasas like Bou Inania. Our drivers are locals who know the best routes and can offer valuable tips on where to eat and shop. Enjoy the authentic atmosphere of Fes with the modern luxury of our fleet.
